## 🚀 What I Do

### 1. Penetration Tester
I perform authorized security testing on networks, systems, and web applications. I follow industry frameworks like **OWASP, PTES, NIST SP 800-115, and MITRE ATT&CK**, as well as the CEH stages:  
*Permission → Reconnaissance → Scanning → Gaining Access → Maintaining Access → Clearing Tracks → Reporting → Communication.*

### 2. Bug Bounty Hunter
I hunt for real-world vulnerabilities on platforms like **HackerOne, Bugcrowd, Intigriti, Synack, and YesWeHack**. My main focus is on high-impact web vulnerabilities including XSS, SQLi, IDOR, SSRF, and business logic flaws.

### 3. Offensive Security Red Teamer
I simulate adversary tactics to evaluate people, processes, and technologies holistically — including social engineering simulations, wireless security, and physical boundary assessments where scope permits.

## 🔄 My Bug Bounty Workflow — Step by Step

This is the exact structured sequence I execute on every target:

1. **Scope and Rules:** Confirm in-scope assets and program policies. Zero testing without permission.
2. **Asset Discovery & OSINT Recon:** `Amass`, `Subfinder`, `theHarvester`, `Shodan`, `Google Dorks`
3. **Subdomain & URL Enumeration:** `Sublist3r`, `Gobuster`, `FFuF`, `Feroxbuster`, `Dirsearch`
4. **Fingerprinting & Mapping:** `Nmap`, `httpx`, `WhatWeb`, `Wappalyzer`
5. **Automated Vulnerability Scanning:** `Nuclei`, `OWASP ZAP`, `Nikto`, `Nessus Essentials/Pro`, `OpenVAS`
6. **Manual Validation:** `Burp Suite Pro/Community`, `Caido`, `SQLmap`, `XSStrike`, `Dalfox`
7. **Proof-of-Concept Exploitation:** `Metasploit Framework`, `SearchSploit`, Custom Python/Bash scripts
8. **Post-Exploitation (Authorized/Lab):** `BloodHound`, `CrackMapExec / NetExec`, `Impacket`, `Mimikatz`
9. **Reporting:** `Dradis`, `Ghostwriter`, Markdown Executive Summaries
10. **Submission:** Clear reproduction steps, impact assessment, and remediation guidance.
